This project is a lot of things and has been responsible for a lot of my growth as a developer, especially early on. The project started as a little Discord bot with a dashboard and has developed into a solid automation and custom scripting platform since. I became involved with the project a little later into its early development (around 2020), when it was in its "v8" phase. At this point the team of enthusiast developers had numbered into a small group of 3-4 people.

### rewrite

After I had made some contributions on both the backend and frontend of things, we started brainstorming a major rewrite of the project through 2021-2022, which would involve moving a *lot* of the infrastructure and backend to Rust (spearheaded by the amazing [sylver](https://sylver.me)). Finally, in early 2022, we started rolling out "vx" (we skipped v9 because referring to it as "vx" was cooler), which featured a complete overhaul and a ton of focus on the scripting experience, involving a scripting language called pella (which is related to [pellagraph](/projects/pellagraph), a node-based editor that allows for visual programming).

I was involved with a lot of the design work (designed the new logo), and also contributed a lot of feature additions, testing and bug fixing across the API, frontend, and backend.

![Atlas dashboard](/assets/projects/atlas/showcase.png)

### disengagement

My contributions since starting Uni in 2023 have been minimal or non-existent for a few of reasons. I still very much respect and admire the people behind the project, and I think it still holds a lot of utility for communities on Discord (a good 100,000+ strong), but my opinion towards Discord as a development platform have shifted significantly over the years, partly due to some historically disappointing developer communication efforts. For the most part, my interests don't really align with the project anymore, and I'm pursuing other things that I find more interesting and fulfilling.

### source

The project itself and most of its architectural components are closed-source, but there have been some efforts to [open source](https://github.com/sylv/atlas) some of it (and maybe later, most of it).
